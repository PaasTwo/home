The PaaS2 operations assistant reads metrics, detects anomalies, and proposes or runs self-healing protocols — pod restarts, cache clears, and similar playbooks.

This does not remove humans from the loop. It is a virtual senior engineer watching the cluster 24/7 so your team can stay on product work.
