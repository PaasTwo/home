title: "Introducing Obsidian Flux"
date: 2026-08-15
description: "The new design system behind the PaaS2 homepage."
author: "PaaS2 team"
---

The PaaS2 homepage is rebuilt on **Obsidian Flux**: a void palette, purple-to-cyan gradients, glass cards, and technical typography.

The intent is control, reliability, and innovation for developers — without visual noise.

Later releases will carry the same language into the panels and docs.
